- `MobMate-v1-e112427-MIT.txt`: the original LICENSE from the previously published
  [`zufall-upon/MobMate` at `e112427f88d2952fee9aef9f0fefc5b3cb6fb328`](https://github.com/zufall-upon/MobMate/blob/e112427f88d2952fee9aef9f0fefc5b3cb6fb328/LICENSE).
  The Git blob ID is `29da43314dcd9fd16b760cffeba4f31a814a11e5`.
  This record preserves the rights and terms of V1; it does not mean that the old MIT license applies
  wholesale to the newly developed V2. Similar functionality alone does not demonstrate a port or copy.
  Third-party materials and the rights that can actually be licensed require separate confirmation.
- `Apache-2.0.txt`: the Apache License 2.0 text for the included `libs/sherpa-onnx/include/c-api.h`.
  See the header and `../THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` for copyright notices.
- `native/vendor/miniaudio.h` contains the original MIT No Attribution / Unlicense alternative texts at its end.
  `libs/onnxruntime/include/` contains Microsoft's MIT notice, and the MIT text is in `../THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
- `Piper-Plus-original-license.txt`: the original `libs/piper_plus/LICENSE.md` from the original candidate.
  `libs/piper_plus_cli/linux/LICENSE.md` is identical. Executables are not included.
- `PulseAudio-original-copyright.txt`: the original `libs/pactl/linux-x64/COPYRIGHT.pulseaudio-utils` from the original candidate.
  The pactl binary is not included.
- `Tsukuyomi-RVC-original-notice.txt`: the original `models/voiceconv/tsukuyomi-rvc/license.txt` from the original candidate.
  “bundle” in that text describes the original distribution; this candidate does not include the model.
  Check the terms at the official URL in the text.

`../THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` preserves the notices from the source snapshot unchanged.
It does not mean every listed item is included, and the main application's GPL or a separate license does not change third-party terms.
For Moonshine, the original notice's description of non-English models differs from the list covered by the
[upstream LICENSE at the pinned revision](https://github.com/moonshine-ai/moonshine/blob/d8d8fa29d05ca67441a7719e70099f9f48917a93/LICENSE).
When acquiring or distributing a model, check the conditions of the applicable version separately.
