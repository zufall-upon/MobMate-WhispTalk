# External PR Relicensing Consent — Draft v0.1

[English](CONTRIBUTING.md) | [日本語](CONTRIBUTING.ja.md) | [简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md)

This is a draft of the procedure and wording. The legal name of the entity receiving the license, contributors' authority,
and the legal validity of the wording have not been confirmed. Confirm them before starting formal operation.
This document itself does not establish anyone's consent or an agreement.

This consent is not required for forks, private modifications, or use and redistribution under the GPL.
Only for **external PRs** to be merged into the main application, the policy is to obtain express consent from the relevant contributors before merging.
Declining consent does not change GPL rights, including the right to fork, but the PR will not be merged into the main application under this policy.
Copyright remains with the contributors.

> Copyright remains with me. I do not grant rights that I do not hold in portions owned by third parties.
> PR: [URL or number]. Commits: [list of full SHAs]. Consent text version: v0.1.

## Minimum record before merging

1. Determine the receiving entity and specify the PR, commit list, and consent text version on the PR.
2. Record explicit consent by each copyright holder or authorized representative for their own contribution, for example in PR comments,
   associating contributor, time, consent text version, PR, and full commit SHAs. No bot is required.
   If authority involving an employer or co-author, for example, is unclear, confirm it and do not merge while unresolved.
3. If commits are added or changed after consent, review the changes and each rights holder, update the covered SHAs,
   and obtain any necessary renewed consent. Also retain the mapping between the merged commits and the record.
4. Preserve the terms and notices for third-party code, models, and so on; do not treat consent alone as obtaining third-party rights.
   Separately confirm the scope for which separate licenses can be granted.
